# 🛡️ BHAROSA — Digital Identity Protection

> **Your Identity. Your Data. Your Control.**

BHAROSA is a blockchain-based digital identity protection system designed to give users greater visibility and control over how their digital identity and personal information are accessed.

The system focuses on answering four important questions:

**Who accessed my identity?  
What information did they access?  
Why did they access it?  
Was the access authorized?**

BHAROSA also aims to detect suspicious or unauthorized access attempts and notify the user.

---

## 🚨 Problem

With the increasing use of digital services, users share their personal and identity information with multiple organizations and platforms.

However, users often have limited visibility into:

- Who accessed their identity
- What information was accessed
- Why the information was accessed
- Whether the access was authorized
- Whether someone attempted unauthorized access
- What happened to their access records

This can increase the risk of identity misuse, impersonation, fraud, and unauthorized access.

The problem becomes even more significant for people with limited digital literacy, particularly grassroots and rural users.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

BHAROSA acts as a **security and transparency layer for digital identity**.

Whenever an organization or service requests access to a user's identity, BHAROSA can record and monitor the access event.

The system is designed to provide the user with a clear activity history showing:

| Information | Example |
|---|---|
| Who | ABC Bank |
| What | Identity Verification |
| Why | Loan Application |
| When | 10:30 AM |
| Authorization | Authorized |
| Status | Successful |

Suspicious or unauthorized activity can be flagged and the user can be alerted.

---

## 🔐 Why Blockchain?

Traditional databases can be modified by authorized administrators.

BHAROSA uses blockchain technology to create a **tamper-resistant audit trail** for identity-access events.

Sensitive personal information is **not intended to be stored directly on the blockchain**.

Instead, cryptographic hashes can be used to create a digital fingerprint of an access record.

### Basic Flow

```text
Identity Access Event
        ↓
Create Access Record
        ↓
Generate Cryptographic Hash
        ↓
Store Proof on Blockchain
        ↓
Tamper-Resistant Audit Trail
        ↓
User Verification
```
